## Quarter 2 Project Proposal

**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

The most interesting topic to me is using Natural Language Processing (NLP) to categorize financial transactions. I find it interesting that machine learning models can identify spending patterns from short and sometimes ambiguous transaction descriptions, which could provide useful information for credit risk assessment.

**2. Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

I would like to investigate whether using NLP-based spending categories can improve credit risk prediction compared to using numerical transaction features alone. I would compare different feature sets to evaluate whether transaction descriptions provide additional predictive value.

**3. What is a potential change you'd make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

One potential change would be to improve the manual labeling process and develop a more consistent approach for handling ambiguous transactions. Some merchant descriptions do not provide enough information to determine the exact spending category, so I would consider introducing an uncertainty label or using confidence thresholds for further manual review.

**4. What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

I would be interested in exploring transformer-based models such as BERT for transaction classification and comparing their performance with simpler models such as FastText. I would also consider using clustering techniques to identify spending patterns and discover potential categories.
